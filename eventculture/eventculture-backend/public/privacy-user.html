Introduction & Overview

Welcome to EventCulture (referred to herein as "EventCulture", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the EventCulture Attendee Mobile Application (Package / Scheme: eventculture-user).

EventCulture is engineered to make discovering, booking, and entering live cultural, academic, gaming, and musical events effortless. We respect your fundamental right to digital privacy and provide this policy to clearly describe what data is collected, why it is needed, how it is secured, and how you retain full control over your personal information.

By downloading, installing, registering on, or using the EventCulture Attendee Application, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to authenticate your account, fulfill ticket reservations, and generate authorized entry passes.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Profile & Authentication Details: Name, email address, phone number (used for secure OTP verification), and optional profile avatar image.
  • Registration Details: Academic institution, department, or student identity numbers if required by a specific event host for eligibility checks.
  • Transaction Metadata: Pass selection, tier purchased (e.g., General Access, VIP, Kit Inclusive), payment transaction reference IDs, and timestamp. (Note: We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers or bank CVVs; payments are processed directly via PCI-DSS certified gateways).
  • Support Queries & Feedback: Content of communications submitted through our in-app support channels.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Telemetry Data: Mobile device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), unique device identifiers, and crash diagnostic logs to ensure application stability.
  • App Usage Analytics: Pages visited, events bookmarked, and notification click-throughs to improve application usability.

How We Use Your Data & Legal Basis

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Pass Issuance & Access Verification: Generating unique, cryptographically signed dynamic QR codes for swift admission at venue gates.
  • Authentication & Security: Verifying your phone number and email via Time-based One-Time Passwords (OTP) to prevent ticket scalping and identity fraud.
  • Customer Service: Assisting you with ticket transfers, refunds, or technical inquiries.
  • Event Communication: Sending critical venue updates, emergency alerts, parking guidelines, or itinerary modifications.
5

QR Passes & Offline Storage Technology

EventCulture features our proprietary One Scan. Zero Queues. pass architecture:

  • Cryptographic Digital Signatures: Every digital QR pass issued to your app contains an encrypted payload verified against event-specific public keys.
  • Offline Gate Ready: Digital passes are saved locally on your device in secure encrypted storage. Even if cellular connectivity or venue Wi-Fi fails at stadium or auditorium gates, your QR pass remains accessible and verifiable.
  • Scan Audit Logs: When a gate volunteer scans your pass, the scan timestamp, gate ID, and validator ID are recorded to ensure passes cannot be duplicated or reused.
6

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

Zero Ad Tracking: EventCulture does NOT sell, rent, or lease attendee personal data to advertising networks, brokers, or data aggregators.

Data is shared strictly under the following controlled circumstances:

  • Verified Event Organizers: When you register for an event, the verified event organizer receives attendee roster information (Name, Email, Pass Category, Custom Questions) necessary for event coordination, safety capacity, and participant certification.
  • Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (AWS / Cloudflare), SMS/Email delivery gateways (Twilio / SendGrid), and payment processors bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where required by law enforcement or competent judicial authorities pursuant to valid court orders.
7

Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with pass records. You retain permanent ownership of your data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

  1. In-App Self-Service: Navigate to Profile > Settings > Security & Privacy > Delete Account. Confirming this action permanently deletes your profile and authentication records.
  2. Direct Request via Email: Email our Data Protection Officer at support@eventculture.in with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. We process and purge all non-statutory records within 14 business days.
8

Data Security & Encryption

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and EventCulture servers are secured with TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database assets and sensitive fields are encrypted using AES-256 standards.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control: Backend operational access is strictly limited to authorized personnel using multi-factor authentication.
9

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The EventCulture User App is not intended for unsupervised use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental or school guardian consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

10

Your Statutory Rights (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP Act)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you enjoy specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Obtain a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used machine-readable format.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of your profile data where retention is no longer legally mandated.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Opt-out of non-essential analytical processing.
