Scope & Contractual Relationship

This Privacy and Data Governance Policy governs the collection, processing, and handling of information collected via the EventCulture Organizer Mobile Application (Package / Scheme: eventculture-organizer) and associated Organizer Portal services.

Throughout this document, "Organizer", "Host", "You", and "Your" refer to event directors, college committee leads, fest coordinators, corporate event managers, or authorized organizational representatives using EventCulture Organizer.

By registering as an Organizer on EventCulture, you agree to this Privacy Policy alongside our Organizer Terms of Service and Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

2

Organizer Information We Collect

A. Organization & Representative Details

  • Identity & Account Details: Representative Name, official email address, phone number (OTP verified), organization/club/institution name, and official designation.
  • Verification & KYC Credentials: Institutional authorization letters, student council accreditations, or business tax/registration documents required to prevent fraudulent event hosting.
  • Event Setup & Creative Assets: Event titles, descriptions, venue coordinates, dates, custom registration questions, banner artworks, and stage layout maps.

B. Financial & Payout Information

  • Bank account numbers, IFSC/SWIFT codes, UPI handles, or payout settlement routing details collected solely for disbursing net ticket revenue.

4

Participant Rosters & CSV Import Processing

When you utilize our Bulk Participant Import feature:

  • Transient File Handling: Uploaded CSV files are processed in-memory, validated against data schemas, converted to encrypted pass records in the EventCulture database, and purged from temporary server buffers.
  • Organizer Ownership: Participant lists uploaded by you remain your intellectual property. EventCulture does not cross-market, sell, or advertise other unrelated events to your private attendee lists without independent attendee consent.
  • Export Security: When you export gate scan analytics or registration sheets, the generated files are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted streams.
5

Volunteer Delegation & Access Control Governance

The Organizer App provides granular access delegation tools to maintain operational security at live venues:

  • Dynamic Event Access PINs: You can generate, assign, and immediately revoke 6-digit Volunteer PIN codes for specific checkpoints (e.g., Gate 1, VIP Lounge, Kit Counter, Food Hall).
  • Zero Financial Leakage: Volunteer tokens only grant pass validation privileges; staff members cannot view organizer revenues, bank accounts, or sensitive private emails.
  • Comprehensive Audit Trail: Every pass scan is tagged with the volunteer ID, gate ID, and exact timestamp for post-event discrepancy reconciliation.
6

Revenue, Passes & Settlement Transparency

We maintain stringent financial compliance:

  • Tokenized Banking: Payout accounts are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Audit Records: Transaction ledgers, platform fee breakdowns, GST/Tax deductions, and refund histories are archived in accordance with statutory accounting and tax compliance laws for a period of up to 7 years.
7

Data Processor Obligations (GDPR / DPDP)

Roles & Responsibilities: Under applicable data protection legislation (such as the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), the Organizer acts as Data Controller for participant lists, while EventCulture acts as Data Processor.

As Data Processor, EventCulture commits to:

  1. Processing participant data strictly in accordance with your documented instructions and configuration within the platform.
  2. Assisting you in fulfilling Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) and erasure requests from attendees.
  3. Promptly notifying you within 48 hours in the unlikely event of any confirmed security incident affecting your event data.
8

Infrastructure Security & Multi-Tenancy

EventCulture operates on an isolated multi-tenant architecture:

  • Logical Tenant Isolation: Strict database filtering ensures no organizer can access or query another organizer's backend records or attendee rosters.
  • API Rate Limiting & Helmet Guard: Express backend protected by Helmet security headers, CORS restrictions, and DDoS mitigation.
  • Session Expiry: Organizer tokens automatically expire and require secure re-authentication to prevent unauthorized access on shared mobile devices.
9

Data Retention & Account Closure

You may request full closure of your organizer account and purge of past event archives:

  • Event archives and attendee rosters can be exported or purged after all financial settlements for concluded events have been finalized.
  • To request account termination, email organizer-support@eventculture.in. Non-statutory records are wiped within 30 days.
