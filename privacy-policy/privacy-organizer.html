Scope & Contractual Relationship

This Privacy and Data Governance Policy governs the collection, processing, and handling of information collected via the EventCulture Organizer Mobile Application (Package / Scheme: eventculture-organizer) and associated Organizer Portal services.

By registering as an Organizer on EventCulture, you agree to this Privacy Policy alongside our Organizer Terms of Service and Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

2

Organizer Information We Collect

A. Organization & Representative Details

  • Identity & Account Details: Representative Name, official email address, phone number (OTP verified), organization/club/institution name, and official designation.
  • Verification & KYC Credentials: Institutional authorization letters, student council accreditations, or business tax/registration documents.
  • Event Setup & Creative Assets: Event titles, descriptions, venue coordinates, dates, custom registration questions, banner artworks, and stage layout maps.

B. Financial & Payout Information

  • Bank account numbers, IFSC/SWIFT codes, UPI handles, or payout settlement routing details collected solely for disbursing net ticket revenue.

4

Participant Rosters & CSV Import Processing

  • Transient File Handling: Uploaded CSV files are parsed in-memory, converted to encrypted pass records, and discarded from temporary server memory.
  • Organizer Ownership: Participant lists uploaded by you remain your intellectual property. We do not cross-market to your attendees.
5

Volunteer Delegation & Access Control Governance

  • Dynamic Event Access PINs: Generate and instantly revoke 6-digit Volunteer PIN codes for specific checkpoints.
  • Zero Financial Leakage: Staff members cannot view organizer revenues or attendee private phone numbers.
6

Revenue, Passes & Settlement Transparency

All payout accounts are tokenized using AES-256 encryption. Financial ledgers are maintained in compliance with statutory taxation laws.

7

Data Processor Obligations (GDPR / DPDP)

Roles & Responsibilities: The Organizer acts as Data Controller, while EventCulture acts as Data Processor.

8

Infrastructure Security & Multi-Tenancy

Strict database tenant isolation prevents cross-organizer data access. All traffic is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption.

9

Data Retention & Account Closure

To terminate your organizer account and purge concluded event records, email organizer-support@eventculture.in.
