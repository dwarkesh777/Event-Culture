Introduction & App Scope

This Privacy Policy specifically addresses the data handling policies of the EventCulture Volunteer Mobile Application (Package / Scheme: eventculture-volunteer).

The Volunteer App is engineered exclusively for authorized event crew members, security personnel, ticketing desks, and student volunteers tasked with high-throughput ticket scanning and entry checkpoint validation (One Scan. Zero Queues.).

2

Camera & Sensor Permissions (Crucial Notice)

Official Camera Disclosure: "EventCulture requires camera access to scan attendee digital QR passes." The camera stream is utilized exclusively to optically detect and decode 2D QR codes and barcodes displayed by attendees.

  • No Photograph or Video Capture: The app does NOT capture, take, or save still photos or video frames.
  • Real-Time Edge Computer Vision: Camera image buffers are parsed transiently in volatile RAM via expo-camera and discarded immediately.
  • Zero Facial Recognition: Zero biometric analysis or face profiling.
  • Audio & Haptic Feedback: Auditory tones and tactile pulses confirm valid or rejected scans.
3

Information Collected from Volunteers

  • Volunteer Credentials: Volunteer Name or Call-Sign, Event Access PIN, assigned Checkpoint ID.
  • Telemetry: Device OS model, battery level, network latency.
  • Scan Audit Timestamps: Exact UTC timestamp and result status.
4

QR Scanning & Attendee Privacy Protection

Personal attendee emails and payment details are never displayed on the volunteer gate scanner screen.

5

Offline Caching & Reconciliation

Scans during venue network drops are queued in encrypted local storage and synchronized automatically once network connection is recovered.

6

Ground Security & Token Expiration

Volunteer PIN access sessions automatically terminate upon event completion or instant organizer revocation.

7

Third-Party Disclosures

Zero advertising trackers or data brokers.

8

App Store & Google Play Developer Policy Compliance

Complies with Google Play Prominent Disclosure & Consent and Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1.
