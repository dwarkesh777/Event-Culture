Introduction & App Scope

This Privacy Policy specifically addresses the data handling policies of the EventCulture Volunteer Mobile Application (Package / Scheme: eventculture-volunteer).

The Volunteer App is engineered exclusively for authorized event crew members, security personnel, ticketing desks, and student volunteers tasked with high-throughput ticket scanning and entry checkpoint validation (One Scan. Zero Queues.).

2

Camera & Sensor Permissions (Crucial Notice)

Official Camera Disclosure: "EventCulture requires camera access to scan attendee digital QR passes." The camera stream is utilized exclusively to optically detect and decode 2D QR codes and barcodes displayed by attendees.

We provide the following unambiguous technical guarantees regarding Camera and Sensor usage:

  • No Photograph or Video Capture: The app does NOT capture, take, or save still photos or video frames to your device storage or our remote servers.
  • Real-Time Edge Computer Vision: Camera image buffers are parsed transiently in volatile device memory (RAM) via expo-camera and immediately discarded once the QR string payload is decoded.
  • Zero Facial Recognition or Biometrics: The Volunteer App does not scan faces, record facial geometry, or perform any biometric identification.
  • Audio & Haptic Feedback (RECORD_AUDIO / Vibration): Audio and haptics permissions are utilized to play affirmative/rejection auditory tones and trigger tactile vibration pulses to confirm valid/invalid scans in noisy auditorium environments.

3

Information Collected from Volunteers

Because the Volunteer App is designed for rapid event duty, we practice extreme data minimization:

  • Volunteer Credentials: Volunteer Name or Station Call-Sign, Event Access PIN, assigned Checkpoint ID (e.g., Gate 3 - North Wing).
  • Device Diagnostic Telemetry: Device OS model, battery level, network latency, and camera initialization latency to diagnose hardware bottlenecks during heavy check-in rushes.
  • Scan Audit Timestamps: Exact UTC timestamp when a pass scan occurred and the resulting status (e.g., APPROVED, ALREADY_USED, INVALID_GATE).
4

QR Scanning & Attendee Privacy Protection

To protect attendee confidentiality during gate operations:

  • Privacy Masking: When a QR pass is scanned, the volunteer interface only reveals the attendee's name, ticket category (e.g., VIP Pass, General Delegate), and access authorization. Personal contact numbers and payment information are masked and inaccessible.
  • Duplicate Prevention: The backend instantly marks passes as redeemed. If a second attempt is made with the same pass, an unmistakable red warning alert is triggered.
5

Offline Caching & Reconciliation

To withstand severe venue network congestion or dead zones:

  • Local Checkpoint Ledger: Scans performed during network drops are temporarily queued in AES-encrypted local cache.
  • Automated Reconnection Sync: As soon as cellular or local Wi-Fi connectivity is restored, queued scan records are automatically dispatched to the server to synchronize overall festival attendance counts.
6

Ground Security & Token Expiration

Volunteer PIN access sessions automatically terminate upon the conclusion of the event window, or immediately when revoked by the lead organizer from the Organizer Console.

7

Third-Party Disclosures

The Volunteer App contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or commercial telemetry brokers. Scan telemetry is transmitted exclusively to EventCulture secure cloud endpoints.

8

This policy complies with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines Section 5.1.1 (Data Collection & Storage): Zero unnecessary background data extraction.
